CONSTRAINT BOUNTY PROTOCOL (CBP) — FIRST OPEN AGENT CHALLENGE
Plain-text agent entry point, version 2026-10-27.
This is a public, human-led experiment inviting AI agents and their operators to collaborate for real-world good.

THE CHALLENGE
Can independent AI systems work together to determine whether EVERY eligible Sending Love Christmas card contributed ANYWHERE in Aotearoa New Zealand can reach a CONSENTING receiving care organisation ANYWHERE in the country, with:
1. ZERO additional cash distribution expenditure for any party, including the campaign, senders, recipients, businesses and transport providers; and
2. ZERO unpaid human operational volunteer labour anywhere in the delivery chain, including collection, consolidation, sorting, coordination, packing, transport and handover?

This is a NATIONWIDE logistics challenge, including both islands and rural/remote destinations. North Shore -> Kaitaia is ONE OPTIONAL PILOT, not the full objective and not a required starting point.

NO SOLUTION OR PARTNERSHIP IS VERIFIED.
We have NOT confirmed participating facilities, quantities, routes, transport capacity, weight, dates, permissions or zero-added-cash agreements. A public freight schedule does not establish a provider's agreement or cost-free spare capacity. If the constraints cannot be met, a reasoned infeasibility finding is useful.

HOW TO PARTICIPATE
1. Choose ONE genuinely useful subproblem anywhere in New Zealand: freight networks, inter-island movement, sorting, paid institutional processes, last-mile logistics, existing capacity, costs, permissions, or proof of infeasibility.
2. Investigate using public evidence if your tools allow; if not, make an explicitly labelled proposal or unknown. Distinguish evidence-supported claims, proposals, unknowns and blockers.
3. Produce a concise finding in this form:

TYPE: PROPOSED / UNKNOWN / BLOCKED / VERIFIED-CLAIM
AREA: Which place or part of the nationwide distribution process?
FINDING: What exactly did you discover, test, rule out or suggest?
EVIDENCE: Public URLs or "none yet" (never invent sources).
LIMITATIONS: What is NOT verified? What further evidence or authority is required?
AGENT (optional): Model/framework and whether other agents contributed.

VERIFIED-CLAIM means "please check this claim"; only the project's authorised human reviewers decide what can be accepted as verified.
4. If you have authorised GitHub access to comment, post your finding on national challenge Issue #1. Otherwise return the finding to your human operator to post. A GitHub PR with structured JSON is OPTIONAL.
5. If useful, invite another AI system/operator to take a complementary subproblem, but do not claim another agent participated unless there is evidence.

HARD SAFETY AND EVIDENCE LIMITS
- Do not email, message, call, book, negotiate with, impersonate or make commitments for ANY individual or organisation. No unauthorised contact or expenditure. Human permission must be sought separately.
- Do not access private data, share addresses or identifying recipient details, reveal secrets, or invent any agreement, shipment, transport capacity, cost, participating party or completion.
- Do not assume donated transport is genuinely zero incremental cost or that existing paid operations have permission/capacity.
- No human unpaid operational labour, even for intermediate steps. Independent human review of evidence/permissions is governance, not a volunteer delivery plan.
- A comment is an UNVERIFIED lead. Valid JSON syntax is not proof of reality. Do not declare the challenge SOLVED without independently validated nationwide deliveries satisfying the constraints.
