# Companion research track: one complete route, tested honestly

**Research status: OPEN — no route demonstrated or authorised.**

**Parent moonshot:** [National CBP Experiment 001](../challenges/000_national_card_distribution.md) and [Issue #1](https://github.com/hkrodgers-eng/constraint-bounty-protocol/issues/1). The **nationwide mission and its authoritative manifest remain unchanged**. A working single route is evidence of local feasibility, **not** national success. A failing single route is not proof every possible route is impossible.

## Why this test exists

Before proposing a national network, examine whether **even one complete real-world route** can satisfy every original condition. The experiment explicitly accepts a precise, evidence-backed failure or an honest unknown as a valuable result. No rule is weakened to manufacture a success.

**Research question:** For one specific origin and one consenting receiving organisation in Aotearoa, can a completed handwritten card travel **from the moment writing is finished to verified handover**, with:

- **Zero additional cash expenditure for anyone**, including writer, Sending Love, host, receiving organisation, logistics provider or other third party, across the entire distribution chain (not merely zero charge to Sending Love).
- **Zero unpaid human operational labour**, including preparing, packing, addressing, collection, consolidation, sorting, quality checks, transfers, routing, loading, coordination, last mile, acceptance, and handover. The act of **writing the message** is outside the distribution chain; any handling performed *after* writing must be counted.
- **Documented consent, safe handling, and lawful/contractually permissible carriage** for every necessary organisation and step. Existing paid routes do not imply free capacity or approval.
- **A genuine start-to-finish chain**, with no invisible handoff, unpublished private recipient information, imaginary volumes or evidence.
- **Compatibility with Sending Love's optional, low-friction participation:** a candidate that *requires* contributor registration, paid postage, purchased materials or digital card photos is not compatible with current campaign practice. Report it as such rather than silently changing Sending Love.

No shipping, contact or operational trial is authorised by this research track.

## Two distinct gates — do not merge them

### Gate A: documented end-to-end feasibility (desk research)

Select **one geographically specific origin-to-destination candidate** only after finding appropriate public leads. The existing [North Shore → Kaitāia case](../challenges/001_kaitaia_freight.md) is an **optional starting hypothesis**, not an established origin, recipient or approved route.

Map every activity and handoff. For each, distinguish public proof from assertion; identify who would do the work, on what paid basis, whether the activity adds expenditure anywhere, and whether separate permission is required. Where private permissions, recipient consent or financial records would be needed, mark **REQUIRES_AUTHORIZATION** or **UNKNOWN**, not verified. A route that looks promising on public evidence alone remains **a candidate**, not "works".

### Gate B: a completed, independently checked delivery (only if separately authorised)

Only a human-authorised real-world test could establish that the proposed route *actually works*. It would require privately reviewed recipient consent, each actor's permission, applicable safety/contract checks, credible evidence of zero extra cash spend and zero unpaid operational work, and a privacy-preserving delivery receipt. **Agents may not initiate contacts, spend, recruit people, arrange transport or conduct the test.** Gate B is *not scheduled or approved*. Normal Sending Love operations are not changed.

## Decision rules and failure tests

1. **PASS (Gate A, verified feasibility):** independent human review confirms a complete chain with evidence and explicit relevant approvals, a defensible incremental cash-cost accounting for *every* actor and step, no unpaid operational labour, and a consenting destination. Public-only research normally cannot reach this standard.
2. **PASS (Gate B, actual delivery):** Gate A is satisfied, a *separately authorised* physical trial occurs, and delivery is independently documented without publishing private recipient information. A proposed route or posted comment is never a delivery.
3. **INFEASIBLE FOR THIS ROUTE:** evidence establishes that an unavoidable step violates a non-negotiable constraint and no compliant alternative exists **within that evaluated route**. Identify the decisive step and evidence. Do **not** call the entire national challenge impossible.
4. **BLOCKED / UNKNOWN:** a decisive permission, worker arrangement, cost or handover fact is missing; explain what evidence would resolve it. Absence of evidence is **not** proof of impossibility.
5. **PROMISING PROPOSAL:** specific candidate with some checked links but incomplete gates; record remaining gaps and no success claim.

If repeated routes fail, compare **which constraint** actually fails (cash, unpaid handling, permission, coverage, verifiability) and whether a *minimal relaxation* might change the answer. Such relaxations may be explored **only as separate counterfactual research** and **never** counted as meeting the unchanged moonshot.

## What to measure

- Number of **distinct, independently evidenced** end-to-end route attempts; source quality.
- For each attempt, how many **necessary legs** are verified versus unknown, and the **first decisive blocker**.
- Which absolute constraint fails most frequently and whether failure is structural or merely lack of data.
- Human review effort and evidence-based handoffs between independent AI operators.
- Gate A and Gate B outcomes **separately**, with no success counted before verification.
