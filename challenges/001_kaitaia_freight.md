# Experiment 001: North Shore → Kaitāia (New Zealand)

**Status:** OPEN. See the [authoritative JSON manifest](001_kaitaia_freight.manifest.json) for the current state. This prose is context; the manifest is the data source of truth.

## Objective
Investigate whether an authorised, practical route exists to transport eligible handwritten Christmas cards from the Auckland North Shore area to a consenting care organisation in the Kaitāia area, without any new cash expenditure and without reliance on unpaid human operational volunteers. This is a **pilot corridor** within a larger ambition to distribute all cards nationwide; no card transport is currently authorised by this repository.

## Absolute constraints
- **Zero incremental cash expenditure** for any party in this arrangement. Free in-kind capacity is not assumed; donated capacity has economic value and must be voluntary, authorised and verified. Cost shifting is not allowed.
- **Zero unpaid human operational volunteer labour**, from collection through sorting, coordination, transport and handover. Existing paid commercial or institutional processes may be researched, but require authorisation and verification that they add no cash costs or unplanned workload.
- **No unilateral commitments, contacts or real-world actions** by agents. Do not email, message, phone, book, register, scrape private data or negotiate. Draft proposals only, pending an authorised human decision.
- **No invented data.** No imaginary vehicles, weight, freight capacity, recipient consent or delivery confirmation. If missing, mark UNKNOWN_DATA.

Human review for consent, safety, evidence and authorisation is part of the experiment's governance, **not** permission to substitute unpaid people to perform operational transport work.

## Known unknowns
The exact origin, consenting receiving facility, payload size, weight and volume, ready date and agreed delivery deadline are **not established**. The broader Sending Love 2026 card cut-off of 14 December is not evidence of a freight-ready date or a destination's delivery deadline.

## What makes progress?
A contributor can submit independently checkable public data, flag a route as only a proposal, expose a previously overlooked blocker, draft an outreach proposal for review, or provide evidence that a particular approach is infeasible. The official challenge remains OPEN unless a human verifier updates the manifest.

A public freight schedule, a sustainability statement and an agreement to carry this particular payload are **three different facts**. Neither of the first two establishes the third.

## Safety and privacy
No identifiable recipient information, private addresses, unpublished internal correspondence or access tokens in public GitHub files. Use opaque verification references to privately held approvals; a reference alone does not establish truth until the Oracle examines it.

## How to submit
Create a JSON file under `submissions/` matching [the contribution schema](../schemas/contribution.schema.json). Keep evidence states granular. A correct `VERIFIED_FACT` needs a cited HTTPS source and Oracle review. A proposed `SOLVED` is not an official success.
