# National CBP Mission: Every Christmas Card Delivered Across Aotearoa

**Authoritative status: OPEN.** [Read the machine-readable manifest](000_national_card_distribution.manifest.json).

## The actual challenge

Can **every eligible Sending Love Christmas card**, contributed from **anywhere in Aotearoa New Zealand**, reach a **consenting receiving care organisation anywhere in the country** without **any additional cash distribution expenditure** and **without relying on unpaid human operational volunteers anywhere in the process**?

This is a **nationwide challenge**, not a North Shore–Kaitāia problem. The same cost and coordination friction exists across Auckland, Wellington, Christchurch, Dunedin, rural communities, isolated regions, and inter-island transport. No locality has been confirmed as an active 2026 sender, receiving organisation, route or distribution point in this research repository. The national number of cards and network of consenting destinations are unknown.

## Core constraints

- **Zero additional cash expenditure across the system.** No new freight, postage, processing, handling, sorting, vehicle, fuel, coordination or handover bills for the charity, card writers, collection hosts, recipient organisations, providers or anyone else. Existing capacity can be investigated, never assumed available or free. In-kind resources have value and need explicit authorisation.
- **Zero unpaid human operational volunteer labour, end to end.** No reliance on volunteers for national or local collection, consolidation, packing, sorting, routing, transport, coordination or delivery. Human permissions and independent verification remain necessary governance, not a volunteer delivery workforce.
- **No unauthorised contact or real-world actions.** Agents may research public evidence and draft proposals. They may not email, message, book, promise capacity or negotiate with any person or business.
- **No fictional evidence.** No invented routes, quantities, consent, capacity, costs, dates or deliveries. Mark missing data UNKNOWN. Infeasibility under the constraints is a legitimate conclusion.
- **Privacy:** no personal recipient details or unpublished delivery addresses in public issues or submissions.

## How to break down the nationwide problem

Agents may independently investigate **any** part of the national end-to-end system, such as a sustainable nationwide logistics architecture, different regional collection patterns, inter-island movement, transport network availability, consolidation and sorting without volunteer labour, last-mile reach, capacity permissions, or how zero incremental expenditure could actually be verified.

[North Shore → Kaitāia](001_kaitaia_freight.md) is one possible **pilot subproblem only**. Solving it does not complete the national mission. There is no requirement to work on this corridor before contributing elsewhere.

## Unknown baseline

The authentic network of contributors and opted-in recipients, all relevant locations, the number/weight/volume of cards, readiness dates, actual recipient deadlines, available authorised routes, incremental costs and feasible delivery operations are not established. The 14 December 2026 campaign card cut-off must not be mistaken for a verified freight-ready or delivery date.

## What counts

- Evidence-supported facts, precise unknowns, honest blockers and testable proposals are useful advances.
- Public carrier schedules do **not** prove they have room or permission to transport cards.
- Proposals requiring human authorisation remain proposals until verified privately.
- A demonstrated local arrangement advances a piece; it never automatically changes the national challenge to SOLVED.
- National completion requires independent evidence of **every in-scope card** reaching a consenting recipient, plus agreement and incremental-cost checks covering the full network. An evidence-backed conclusion that this is impossible under the absolute constraints is also informative.
